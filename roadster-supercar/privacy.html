Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what the mobile game Highway Roadster ("the game", "we") collects, why, and what you can do about it. We do not ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, photos or microphone.

1. Data stored only on your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, missions, settings, best score) is saved in the game's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Online leaderboard

When you finish a run with an internet connection, the game sends to our leaderboard server:

  • a random player ID and a random secret created on your device (not linked to your device ID, account or identity; we store only a hash of the secret),
  • the player name you choose (or a generated one such as "Pilot1234"),
  • your best score and best distance, and the time they were set.

Your player name and best score are visible to everyone in the in-game leaderboard. Please do not use your real name or anything personal as your player name. Names with offensive words are refused, and other players can report a name; reported names may be hidden.

The leaderboard runs on Supabase (servers in the EU, Frankfurt). Like any web server, it briefly processes your IP address to answer the request; we do not keep it with your leaderboard entry.

Delete your leaderboard data: in the game open Settings → About & privacy → Delete leaderboard data. Your entry is deleted from the server immediately and the game gives you a new random identity. You can also e-mail us (see Contact).

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads served through Unity LevelPlay and its partner ad networks. To show and measure ads, these companies may collect device information such as the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device model, OS version, ad interactions and diagnostic data.

  • The first time you open the game we ask whether ads may be personalised. No ads load until you answer, and if you say no they stay non-personalised. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → About & privacy → Personalised ads.
  • On iOS the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your IDFA is not used.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings.

Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. A list of LevelPlay's ad network partners and their policies is available from Unity.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of which plan is active. The purchase system (Unity IAP) processes purchase history, device identifiers, approximate location, and crash and diagnostic data to complete purchases and keep them working.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, KVKK in Türkiye or the CCPA/CPRA in California) you may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your data, and object to its processing. Use the in-game delete option or contact us. We do not sell personal information.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All traffic between the game and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). The leaderboard database can only be changed through checked server functions; it cannot be read or edited directly.

9. Changes

We will update this page when the game's data use changes and change the date at the top.
