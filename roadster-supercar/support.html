Support

Questions, bugs or a problem with a purchase? E-mail nemindco@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and the game version (shown at the bottom of Settings → About & privacy).

My subscription is not active

Open Settings → About & privacy and tap Restore purchases (iOS). On Android the game checks Google Play every time it starts; make sure you are signed in with the account that bought it.

Cancel a subscription

Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Delete my leaderboard data

Settings → About & privacy → Delete leaderboard data. You can also read how to delete your data.

Report an offensive name

In the leaderboard tap the name twice. Names reported by several players are hidden.
