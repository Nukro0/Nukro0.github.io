Delete your data

This page explains how to delete your data from Highway Roadster: Car Racing, published by Nemind. The game has no user accounts.

In the game

  1. Open the game and go to Settings → About & privacy.
  2. Tap Delete leaderboard data and confirm.

Your leaderboard entry is deleted from our server immediately.

By e-mail

If you can no longer open the game, e-mail nemindco@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your player name. We answer within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your random player ID, player name, best score, best distance and the dates they were set.
  • Reports linked to your entry.

What is not deleted here

  • Progress on your phone: coins, upgrades and settings stay on your phone until you uninstall the game.
  • Ad data: Unity LevelPlay and its ad partners hold it under their own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings. See Unity's privacy policy.
  • Purchases: Google Play and the Apple App Store keep purchase records under their own policies.

We keep no copy of a deleted leaderboard entry. More details are in the privacy policy.
