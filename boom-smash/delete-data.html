Delete your data

This page explains how to delete your data from Boom Smash: Fırfır Panayırı, published by Nemind. The Game has no user accounts.

By e-mail

E-mail nemindco@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your in-game name. We delete your leaderboard entry and answer within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your random player ID, in-game name, avatar choice and the progress shown on the leaderboard (levels passed, ribbons, spare balls).

What stays on your phone

Progress, coins and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstall the Game or clear its data in your phone settings to remove them.

Verilerini silmek

Oyunda hesap yoktur. Sıralama kaydının silinmesi için nemindco@gmail.com adresine "Verilerimi silin" konusuyla oyundaki adını yaz; kaydın silinir ve 30 gün içinde yanıt verilir. Telefonundaki ilerleme ve jetonlar oyunu silince ya da uygulama verisini temizleyince silinir.
